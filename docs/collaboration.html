CollaborationNext release

Invite teammates into your Phun over the internet or your network, and choose what each one may do.

In the next release

Collaboration arrives with the next Phun release. This page describes it as it will ship.

Invite a teammate

The Collaborate button in the title bar, or Collaborate: invite or join a teammate in the command palette, invites up to eight people into your Phun. Each one joins with a code.

The code works over the internet, through a temporary Cloudflare tunnel, and on your network or VPN alike: each invite offers your LAN and VPN addresses and the internet route, and your teammate's Phun tries them all at once. Phun fetches cloudflared itself when it is missing.

Let them in

  1. Check the code together

    Before each person gets in, you and they check a six-digit code together.

  2. Choose what they may do

    From watching, through to editing code or opening terminals.

  3. Work together

    Everyone sees everyone's pointer, each in its own colour.

You can change what each person may do, remove one, or end the session at any time.

Privacy

Everything between the Phuns is end-to-end encrypted.
